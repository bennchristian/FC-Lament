# KNOWN

**Empathy first. Then prayer.**

KNOWN is a private, in-the-moment Scripture experience for someone carrying something hard far from home. It works in five steps:

1. She names what she's carrying.
2. She meets someone in Scripture who carried something similar.
3. She reads their own words.
4. She notices what resonates.
5. She chooses a gentle response: pray, sit with it, reflect, or share with someone. To stop, she closes KNOWN.

KNOWN offers language. She decides what fits.

## Who it's for

Our primary persona is **Esther**, an international Christian student living away from familiar support. She is navigating displacement, loneliness or grief. A trusted person from her church or community may introduce KNOWN and sit with her the first time. She can also find it on her own.

## What we're making it for

FC (Frontier Commons) is building KNOWN for the **2026 Gloo AI Hackathon**:

- **Track:** Track 2 · Scripture Beyond the App · Activation lane (track lead: YouVersion + Biblica).
- **Challenge:** our response to FC's *Trauma and Lament* challenge.
- **The lane's question:** how do you meet people with Scripture in the human moments where they already are?

## What KNOWN is not

- Not a journal or mood tracker, and not a streak-based devotional.
- Not therapy or crisis support.
- Not a social network.
- Not an AI that reads her writing or interprets Scripture.
- No account is needed. Nothing she writes is saved or shared unless she chooses to share it.
