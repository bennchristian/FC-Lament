# KNOWN: one prototype from the best of four artifacts

A recommended feature list for a single prototype, built on artifact 2 (the only one that meets every Must) with the strongest ideas from artifacts 1, 3 and 4.

- **Date:** October 1, 2026
- **Based on:** `KNOWN-MoSCoW.md` in this folder
- **Artifacts:** 1 KNOWN (multilingual) · 2 KNOWN walkthrough · 3 Feelings check-in · 4 KNOWN Pathways
- **Status:** *Agreed* means it is already in `PRD.md` or `DESIGN.md`. *Sign-off* means it widens the PRD and needs that owner's approval first. *Team tool* means it helps the team and is not part of what testers see.

This is a recommendation. Nothing marked *Sign-off* goes into the prototype until its owner approves it and `PRD.md` is updated.
